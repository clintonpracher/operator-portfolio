# The Product System Stabilization Framework

A $120M connected-hardware line was losing its season to its own roadmap. Six local roadmaps, no single source of truth, requirements taking two weeks to clarify, and 40% of engineering effort going to rework. In 30 days: one score for the whole organization, discovery moved upstream, features reframed as platform capabilities, and product intent separated from program execution. Clarification dropped to two days. Rework went to zero.

**Best for:** Product operations leadership, operating model and decision architecture
