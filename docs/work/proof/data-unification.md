# From Data Chaos to Confident Decisions

A global marketing organization was making $600M in annual media decisions across 140 markets against 45 disconnected data systems. Every team could compute a defensible number, and no two matched, so meetings became arguments about inputs instead of choices. Consolidating 45 sources into one taxonomy and one governance model unified 95% of the spend onto a single platform and cut decision time by 40%.

**Best for:** Platform / Data / AI product leadership, enterprise decision systems
